# Lucidity

Clear, lucid, and unambiguous output style for human reading.

## Philosophy

AI returns a high-level, overtly overly abstracted vernacular to humans at the end of operational turns.
Lucidity reverses the response, allowing any human to read and understand what the AI is communicating.

### Examples to avoid

**Puffery**: excessive promotional language presented as opinion.
**Embellishment**: dressing up facts beyond what can reasonably be supported, or understood.
**False authority**: presenting speculation as professional certainty.
